How quality is protected

  1. Each image is encoded at several quality levels, from high to low.
  2. Every attempt is decoded again and compared with the original using SSIM, the standard score for how alike two images look. On large images the check samples the most detailed and the smoothest parts, where compression damage shows first.
  3. The smallest file that still scores above the level you picked is kept. Balanced needs a 99.3% match and never drops below quality 80; Max needs 99.6% and never drops below 88.
  4. If the result is not smaller than the original, you get the original back untouched. A file never comes out bigger unless you asked for a different format or turned on AI enhance.

What happens to each format

FormatResult
JPGRe-encoded as JPG at the lowest quality that passes the check.
PNGPacked into an exact color palette when it has 256 colors or fewer (no loss at all), a close palette when that passes the check, or saved losslessly. Transparency is kept.
WebPRe-encoded as WebP. Transparency is kept.
GIFA still GIF is saved as PNG, because browsers cannot write GIF. Animated GIFs are returned unchanged.
BMPSaved as JPG or PNG, whichever is smaller. Usually 90% smaller or more.
AVIFSaved as WebP when that is smaller; AVIF is often already as small as it gets.
SVGThe code is cleaned: comments, editor data, extra spaces and long decimals are removed. The drawing is rendered before and after to confirm it looks the same.
HEICiPhone and Samsung photos. Opened with a built-in decoder (downloaded the first time you add one) and saved as JPG, so they open everywhere. HEIC is already very compact, so the JPG can be larger; pick a Resize size for smaller files.
TIFFOpened with a built-in decoder and saved as JPG, or PNG when it has transparency. Usually much smaller.

Animated PNG and WebP files are returned unchanged so the animation survives. Camera data such as EXIF and GPS location is removed from compressed files, which also keeps your location private. If an image is too big for the memory this browser allows, it is scaled down automatically and tried again.

Compress to 10 KB: questions

How do I compress an image to 10 KB?

Drop your photo on this page. The target is already set to 10 KB, so the tool finds the highest quality and size that fit under 10 KB and gives you a JPG to download.

Will a 10 KB photo look blurry?

To fit 10 KB the tool first lowers the JPG quality a little and, only if needed, the dimensions. It keeps quality at 60 or higher whenever possible and shows a similarity score for every photo, so you can check it before you download.

Can I compress many photos to 10 KB at once?

Yes. Drop as many photos or whole folders as you like. Every one comes out under 10 KB, and you can download them all as one ZIP file.

What if my photo is already smaller than 10 KB?

Then it is returned untouched, so it never loses quality for no reason.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, adds no watermark, and has no limit on the number of images.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

Can I compress iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes. HEIC photos from iPhone and Samsung phones are opened in the browser and saved as JPG.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes. It works in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac.
